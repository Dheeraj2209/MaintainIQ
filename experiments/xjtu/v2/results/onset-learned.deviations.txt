DV1 (task vs protocol; scope). The task asks for "supervised/learned onset detector families in the protocol candidate space".
    The frozen candidate space (section 5 item 1) contains NO supervised/learned onset family: ET-onset was removed (A.8,
    "No exploratory version is pre-registered here"), and 1.4.3 forbids training anything on the onset GT. Because the task
    also says "search ONLY the protocol's candidate space", no supervised onset classifier is trained. The only
    label-driven stage-1 learning the protocol allows is the nested inner selection of the 24 rule configs (ratio/dual-gate
    x self/fallback) by inner C1, which is computed against the offline onset GT of the 14 training bearings (4.3 step 1).
    That is what "onset-learned" (N1) implements. Reported as a scope finding, not hidden.
DV2 (stage 2 fixed, task-directed). Section 4.3 step 2 selects the downstream (672 combos) by nested LOBO. Per the task,
    the downstream is fixed to the protocol's simplest reference = D's downstream {hard latch, TSO, global conformal,
    model_crit q50<=30, S1 2.0, S2 off} so that the stage-1 effect is isolated. Consequently N1 is NOT the protocol's N;
    decision rule 4.4.1 ("N ships") cannot be invoked for N1. All G/P gates are still computed for N1 and are labelled
    EXPLORATORY (stage-1-only track). D and D' are computed exactly as pre-registered (D first, before any candidate).
